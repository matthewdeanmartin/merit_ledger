# The Five Precepts 五戒

The lay precepts of Chinese Buddhism, received after the Three Refuges. In the ordination
liturgy each is given as 「盡形壽不殺生……」 ("for the rest of my life, not to kill…").

**Source.** *Sutra on the Characteristics of the Five Lay Precepts* 佛說優婆塞五戒相經, Taishō
vol. 24 no. 1476, translated by Guṇavarman (求那跋摩) in 431. It is a casebook: for each
precept it sets out what breaks it, what is a lesser, forgivable fault, and what is no fault at all.
The novice ten (`novice-ten.md`) open with the same five, except that a novice's third precept is
celibacy (不婬) where a lay person's is sexual misconduct (不邪婬).

**Taking only some.** The *Upāsakaśīla Sūtra* (T1488) describes lay people taking one, two, three,
four or all five precepts, after the Three Refuges:

> 若受三歸，受持一戒，是名一分；受三歸已，受持二戒，是名少分；若受三歸，持二戒已，若破一戒，是名無分；若受三歸，受持三、四戒，是名多分；若受三歸，受持五戒，是名滿分。……若隨意說，爾時智者當隨意授。
> — T1488, [T24n1488_p1049a18](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049a18) – [T24n1488_p1049a27](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049a27)

Our rendering: *Taking the Refuges and one precept is called "one part"; two precepts, "a small part";
taking two and breaking one, "no part"; three or four, "a large part"; all five, "the full part". …
Whatever the person asks for, the teacher confers.* This is what the board's partial taking
follows.

**Citation:** 佛說優婆塞五戒相經 (Sutra on the Characteristics of the Five Lay Precepts), Taishō vol. 24, no. 1476, tr. Guṇavarman (求那跋摩), 431. Text from CBETA (the Chinese Buddhist Electronic Text Association, Taipei), linked line by line.

## The precepts

### 1. No killing · 不殺生

> 犯殺有三種奪人命

Do not kill.

Source: [T24n1476_p0940a01](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1476_p0940a01)

*Note:* T1476 grades killing: killing a person breaks the precept beyond repair (不可悔); killing an animal is a lesser fault that can be confessed (下可悔), [T24n1476_p0940b05](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1476_p0940b05). The precept itself is not to kill at all. (The Taishō text has no heading for this first section; the later ones are headed 盜戒第二 and so on.)

### 2. No stealing · 不偷盜

> 以三種取他重物

Do not take what is not given.

Source: [T24n1476_p0941c16](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1476_p0941c16)

### 3. No sexual misconduct · 不邪婬

> 是中犯邪婬有四處

Do not engage in sexual misconduct.

Source: [T24n1476_p0942c25](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1476_p0942c25)

*Note:* For lay people the precept is 邪婬, sexual misconduct, not celibacy. Zhiyi's commentary on the bodhisattva precepts says the same of lay recipients, [T40n1811_p0572c02](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T40n1811_p0572c02).

### 4. No lying · 不妄語

> 乃至戲笑尚不應妄語

Do not lie, not even in jest.

Source: [T24n1476_p0943a26](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1476_p0943a26)

*Note:* T1476 treats claiming spiritual attainments one doesn't have as the grave form of lying ([T24n1476_p0943b15](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1476_p0943b15)); other lies are faults that can be confessed.

### 5. No alcohol · 不飲酒

> 不得飲酒乃至小草頭一滴

Do not drink alcohol, not even a drop.

Source: [T24n1476_p0944a13](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1476_p0944a13)

*Note:* T1476 defines alcohol by its colour, smell and taste and its power to intoxicate; a drink that only looks like alcohol, with no smell, taste or power to intoxicate, breaks nothing ([T24n1476_p0944a15](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1476_p0944a15) – a24).
