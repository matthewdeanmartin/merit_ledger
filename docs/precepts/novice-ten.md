# The Ten Novice Precepts 沙彌十戒

The precepts of a novice monk or nun (沙彌, 沙彌尼): the first step of monastic ordination, not
the ten wholesome deeds (十善業). Recording them on the board means the person was ordained
as a novice, or keeps them as a novice would.

**Source.** *The Ten Novice Precepts and Deportment* 沙彌十戒法并威儀, Taishō vol. 24 no.
1471, where the preceptor gives each as 「盡形壽不殺生，持沙彌戒」. We also cite the late-Ming
manual still used in Chinese monasteries, Lianchi Zhuhong's (蓮池袾宏) 沙彌律儀要略, as quoted
in its commentary 沙彌律儀要略增註 (卍續藏 vol. 60 no. 1118). The two agree word for word, except that
the 卍續藏 writes 牀 for 床 in the eighth.

**Citation:** 沙彌十戒法并威儀 (The Ten Novice Precepts and Deportment), Taishō vol. 24, no. 1471; also Zhuhong (蓮池袾宏), 沙彌律儀要略, as quoted in X60 no. 1118. Text from CBETA (the Chinese Buddhist Electronic Text Association, Taipei), linked line by line.

## The precepts

### 1. No killing · 不殺生

> 盡形壽不殺生

Do not kill.

Source: [T24n1471_p0926b13](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1471_p0926b13), [X60n1118_p0228a07](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/X60n1118_p0228a07)

### 2. No stealing · 不盜

> 盡形壽不盜

Do not steal.

Source: [T24n1471_p0926b13](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1471_p0926b13), [X60n1118_p0229c21](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/X60n1118_p0229c21)

### 3. Celibacy · 不婬

> 盡形壽不婬

Do not engage in any sexual activity.

Source: [T24n1471_p0926b14](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1471_p0926b14), [X60n1118_p0231a18](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/X60n1118_p0231a18)

*Note:* For a novice this is complete celibacy, where the lay Five have 不邪婬.

### 4. No lying · 不妄語

> 盡形壽不妄語

Do not lie.

Source: [T24n1471_p0926b14](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1471_p0926b14), [X60n1118_p0232b03](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/X60n1118_p0232b03)

### 5. No alcohol · 不飲酒

> 盡形壽不飲酒

Do not drink alcohol.

Source: [T24n1471_p0926b15](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1471_p0926b15), [X60n1118_p0234a21](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/X60n1118_p0234a21)

### 6. No garlands or perfume · 不著香華鬘不香塗身

> 不著香華鬘不香塗身

Do not wear flower garlands or put scent on the body.

Source: [T24n1471_p0926b16](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1471_p0926b16), [X60n1118_p0235c02](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/X60n1118_p0235c02)

### 7. No shows · 不歌舞倡妓不往觀聽

> 不歌舞倡妓不往觀聽

Do not sing, dance or perform, and do not go to watch or listen to performances.

Source: [T24n1471_p0926b17](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1471_p0926b17), [X60n1118_p0237a13](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/X60n1118_p0237a13)

### 8. No high, wide beds · 不坐高廣大床

> 不坐高廣大床

Do not sit or lie on high, wide, luxurious beds.

Source: [T24n1471_p0926b18](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1471_p0926b18), [X60n1118_p0238b11](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/X60n1118_p0238b11)

### 9. No eating after midday · 不非時食

> 不非時食

Do not eat outside the proper time (from midday until the next dawn).

Source: [T24n1471_p0926b18](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1471_p0926b18), [X60n1118_p0239b06](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/X60n1118_p0239b06)

*Note:* 非時, "the wrong time": from midday until dawn the next day, per Zhuhong's commentary [X60n1118_p0239b08](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/X60n1118_p0239b08) – b11.

### 10. No money or valuables · 不捉持生像金銀寶物

> 不捉持生像金銀寶物

Do not handle gold, silver, money or other valuables.

Source: [T24n1471_p0926b19](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1471_p0926b19), [X60n1118_p0241a24](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/X60n1118_p0241a24)

*Note:* 生像 is gold and silver: the commentary explains 生色 as gold, whose colour is its own, and 似色 as silver, which can be dyed to look like gold, [X60n1118_p0241b04](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/X60n1118_p0241b04) – b06. Monasteries today read it as handling money.
