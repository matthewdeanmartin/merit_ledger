# The Upāsaka Precepts 優婆塞戒

Six major and twenty-eight minor precepts for lay Buddhists, from the chapter on receiving the
precepts (受戒品第十四) of the *Upāsakaśīla Sūtra* 優婆塞戒經, Taishō vol. 24 no. 1488, translated
by Dharmakṣema (曇無讖) around 426. The sūtra speaks to laymen (優婆塞); in Chinese practice
laywomen (優婆夷) take the same precepts.

**How they're taken (as the sūtra describes it).** The candidate is questioned about their way of life,
spends six months learning under a monastic teacher, and then receives the precepts before a sangha
of twenty ([T24n1488_p1049a02](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049a02) – a08). After the Three Refuges they take some or all of the five precepts (the
partial taking in `five.md`), and are then told the six major and twenty-eight minor precepts.

**Practical advice before the precepts.** Among the questions put to the candidate (all from
[T24n1488_p1048c21](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1048c21) – [T24n1488_p1049a01](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049a01)):

> 先學世事，既學通達，如法求財，若得財物，應作四分：一分應供養父母、己身、妻子、眷屬，二分應作如法販博，留餘一分藏積擬用。

Our rendering: *First learn a trade, and earn honestly. Divide what you earn into four: one part
to support your parents, yourself, your spouse and household; two parts for honest business; and
keep one part saved against need.* It goes on: don't leave your wealth with the old, the far
away, the wicked or the powerful, and keep away from four kinds of people: those who like to talk about
others' faults, those who like to argue wrong views, the smooth-tongued and ill-meaning, and those who
say much and do little.

**The minor precepts' formula.** Each minor precept ends 「是優婆塞得失意罪，不起、墮落、不淨、有作」:
the person commits a 失意罪, a fault of losing one's resolve, which "does not rise, falls, is impure,
and has consequences". Below, the formula is shortened to ……. Breaking a major precept loses the
upāsaka precepts altogether ([T24n1488_p1049b01](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049b01)).

**Citation:** 優婆塞戒經・受戒品第十四 (Upāsakaśīla Sūtra, chapter 14: Receiving the Precepts), Taishō vol. 24, no. 1488, tr. Dharmakṣema (曇無讖), c. 426. Text from CBETA (the Chinese Buddhist Electronic Text Association, Taipei), linked line by line.

**Renderings:** `llm-draft`. See [README.md](README.md).

## Major precepts

### 1. No killing · 不殺

> 雖為天女乃至蟻子悉不應殺。若受戒已，若口教殺、若身自殺，是人即失優婆塞戒，是人尚不能得煖法，況須陀洹至阿那含？是名破戒優婆塞、臭優婆塞、旃陀羅優婆塞、垢優婆塞、結優婆塞。是名初重。若破是戒……

Do not kill or have anything killed, not even an ant.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049a28](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049a28)

*Note:* The source: "not to kill even a goddess or an ant". Killing by word (ordering it) counts as well as by hand.

### 2. No stealing · 不偷盜

> 優婆塞戒，雖為身命，不得偷盜乃至一錢。若破是戒……

Do not steal, not even a single coin, even to save your life.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049b04](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049b04)

### 3. No false claims of attainment · 不虛說得聖法

> 優婆塞戒，雖為身命，不得虛說我得不淨觀至阿那含。若破是戒……

Do not falsely claim meditative attainments or stages of awakening, even to save your life.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049b08](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049b08)

*Note:* Lying in general is not one of the six; claiming attainments is. The full text names 不淨觀 (meditation on the unattractive) up to the third stage of awakening (阿那含).

### 4. No sexual misconduct · 不邪婬

> 優婆塞戒，雖為身命，不得邪婬。若破是戒……

Do not engage in sexual misconduct, even to save your life.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049b12](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049b12)

### 5. Not broadcasting others' faults · 不說四眾過

> 優婆塞戒，雖為身命，不得宣說比丘、比丘尼、優婆塞、優婆夷所有過罪。若破是戒……

Do not spread the faults of monks, nuns, laymen or laywomen.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049b16](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049b16)

*Note:* The same precept is the sixth major Brahmajāla precept.

### 6. No selling alcohol · 不酤酒

> 優婆塞戒，雖為身命，不得酤酒。若破是戒……

Do not sell alcohol.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049b20](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049b20)

*Note:* Selling alcohol is a major precept; drinking is minor precept 2.

## Minor precepts

### 1. Support parents and teachers · 供養父母師長

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，不能供養父母、師長，……

Support and honour your parents and teachers.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c01](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c01)

### 2. No indulging in drink · 不耽樂飲酒

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，耽樂飲酒，……

Do not indulge in drinking alcohol.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c03](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c03)

### 3. Care for the sick · 瞻視病苦

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，污惡不能瞻視病苦，……

Do not turn away in distaste from caring for the sick.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c05](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c05)

### 4. Give to those who ask · 隨宜分與乞者

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，見有乞者，不能多少隨宜分與，空遣還者，……

When someone asks for help, give what you suitably can; do not send them away empty-handed.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c07](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c07)

### 5. Greet monastics and elders · 承迎禮拜

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，若見比丘、比丘尼、長老，先宿諸優婆塞、優婆夷等，不起承迎、禮拜、問訊，……

Rise to greet and pay respect to monks, nuns, elders and senior lay practitioners.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c09](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c09)

### 6. No pride over others' lapses · 不生憍慢

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，若見比丘、比丘尼、優婆塞、優婆夷毀所受戒，心生憍慢，言我勝彼，彼不如我，……

When others break their precepts, do not think yourself better than them.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c12](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c12)

### 7. Keep the eight precepts six days a month · 六齋日受八戒

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，月月之中，不能六日受持八戒，供養三寶，……

On six days each month, keep the eight precepts and make offerings to the Three Jewels.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c15](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c15)

*Note:* The eight precepts (八戒, 八關齋戒) kept on the six fasting days of the month: the household's Chinese-inspired calendar marks these days.

### 8. Go to hear the Dharma · 往聽講法

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，四十里中有講法處，不能往聽，……

When the Dharma is taught nearby (the text says within forty li), go and listen.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c17](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c17)

*Note:* Forty li was roughly twenty kilometres.

### 9. Leave the sangha's furnishings be · 不受招提僧臥具

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，受招提僧臥具床座，……

Do not use beds or seats that belong to the whole sangha.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c20](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c20)

*Note:* 招提僧, the sangha of the four directions: property held for all monastics, not for lay use.

### 10. Spare the creatures in water · 不飲有虫水

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，疑水有虫，故便飲之，……

Do not drink water you suspect has living creatures in it.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c21](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c21)

### 11. Don't travel dangerous places alone · 不嶮難獨行

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，嶮難之處無伴獨行，……

Do not travel alone through dangerous places.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c23](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c23)

### 12. Don't stay alone at a nunnery · 不獨宿尼寺

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，獨宿尼寺，……

Do not spend the night alone at a nunnery.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c25](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c25)

*Note:* Written for laymen. The usual reading for laywomen is the mirror image: not staying alone overnight at a monastery of monks.

### 13. Don't beat or abuse workers · 不打罵奴婢

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，為於財命打罵奴婢、僮僕、外人，……

Do not beat or abuse servants, workers or anyone else for the sake of money or a living.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c27](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c27)

### 14. Don't offer leftovers · 不以殘食施

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，若以殘食施於比丘、比丘尼、優婆塞、優婆夷，……

Do not give leftover food to monastics or fellow lay practitioners.

Source: [T24n1488_p1049c29](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1049c29)

### 15. Don't keep cats · 不畜猫狸

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，若畜猫狸，……

Do not keep cats.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050a02](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050a02)

*Note:* Cats hunt. The Brahmajāla has the same concern (minor 32: not raising cats, wildcats, pigs or dogs).

### 16. Don't keep livestock as your own · 畜獸作淨

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，畜養象、馬、牛、羊、駝、驢一切畜獸，不作淨，施未受戒者，……

Do not keep livestock for yourself; formally pass them to someone who has not taken the precepts.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050a04](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050a04)

*Note:* Uncertain. 不作淨，施未受戒者 is usually read as: livestock should be formally made over (作淨) to someone without precepts, rather than kept as one's own. **Owner to confirm the rendering.**

### 17. Keep robe, bowl and staff · 儲畜衣鉢錫杖

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，若不儲畜僧伽梨衣、鉢盂、錫杖，……

Keep a monastic robe, a bowl and a staff in store.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050a07](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050a07)

*Note:* Usually read as keeping these in store to offer to monastics. **Owner to confirm the rendering.**

### 18. Farm with care for creatures · 求淨水陸稼處

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，若為身命須田作者，不求淨水及陸稼處，……

If you farm for a living, look for clean water and dry ground.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050a09](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050a09)

*Note:* Clean water and dry ground mean less killing of small creatures in the soil and water.

### 19. Fair prices and honest weights · 斗秤平用

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，為於身命，若作市易斗秤賣物，一說價已，不得前却捨賤趣貴；斗秤量物任前平用，如其不平，應語令平。若不如是，……

In trade, keep to the price you named and use honest weights and measures; if a scale is off, say so.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050a11](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050a11)

*Note:* The source: once a price is stated, don't back out of it to sell to a higher bidder; weigh and measure fairly, and if the scales are off, say so.

### 20. Right place and time · 不非處非時行欲

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，若於非處、非時行欲，……

Do not have sex in an improper place or at an improper time.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050a15](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050a15)

### 21. Pay your taxes · 輸官稅

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，商估販賣不輸官稅，盜棄去者，……

In business, pay the taxes you owe; do not dodge them.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050a17](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050a17)

*Note:* Tax on goods in trade (官稅). Minor 22 covers the law in general.

### 22. Keep the law · 不犯國制

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，若犯國制，……

Do not break the laws of the land.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050a19](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050a19)

### 23. Offer first fruits · 先獻三寶

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，若得新穀、果苽、菜茹，不先奉獻供養三寶，先自受者，……

When new grain, fruit or vegetables come in, offer some to the Three Jewels before eating them yourself.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050a21](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050a21)

### 24. Teach with the sangha's leave · 僧聽乃說法

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，僧若不聽說法、讚歎，輒自作者，……

Do not preach or lead praises unless the sangha has asked you to.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050a23](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050a23)

### 25. Don't walk ahead of monastics · 不在比丘前行

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，道路若在諸比丘前、沙彌前行，……

On the road, do not walk in front of monks or novices.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050a25](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050a25)

### 26. Serve the sangha fairly · 賦食平等

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，僧中賦食，若偏為師選擇美好，過分與之，……

When serving food to the sangha, do not pick out the best for your own teacher or give them more than their share.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050a28](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050a28)

### 27. Don't raise silkworms · 不養蠶

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，若養蠶者，……

Do not raise silkworms.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050b01](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050b01)

*Note:* Silk is made by boiling the cocoons.

### 28. Don't leave the sick on the road · 瞻視路病

> 若優婆塞受持戒已，行路之時遇見病者，不住瞻視，為作方便，付囑所在而捨去者，……

If you meet someone sick on the road, stop and help, and see them into someone's care before you go on.

Source: [T24n1488_p1050b03](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T24n1488_p1050b03)
