# Evening reflection

Before sleep, look back over the day without judging it.

- Where did I keep my precepts today, and where did I slip?
- Whom did I help? Whom did I hurt, and can I make it right tomorrow?
- What did I learn, and from whom?
- What good did others do that I can rejoice in?

Set down what can't be fixed tonight. Tomorrow is another chance to practise.
