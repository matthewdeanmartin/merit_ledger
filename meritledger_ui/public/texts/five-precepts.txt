# The Five Precepts

1. **No killing** (不殺生). Do not kill.

2. **No stealing** (不偷盜). Do not take what is not given.

3. **No sexual misconduct** (不邪婬). Do not engage in sexual misconduct.

4. **No lying** (不妄語). Do not lie, not even in jest.

5. **No alcohol** (不飲酒). Do not drink alcohol, not even a drop.

The sources and notes are on the board's Precepts page.
