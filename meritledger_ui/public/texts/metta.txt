# Loving-kindness

Sit comfortably. Let the breath settle.

May I be safe. May I be well. May I be at ease. May I be kind to myself.

Bring to mind someone who has helped you. May you be safe. May you be well. May you be at ease.

Bring to mind someone you hardly know: the cashier, a neighbour. May you be safe. May you be well. May you be at ease.

Bring to mind someone you find difficult. As much as you honestly can: may you be safe. May you be well. May you be at ease.

Let it widen to this house, this street, this city, every living thing. May all beings be safe, well and at ease.
