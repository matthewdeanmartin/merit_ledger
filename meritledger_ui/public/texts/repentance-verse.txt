# Verse of repentance

All the harm I have done in the past,
born of greed, anger and confusion that have no beginning,
arising from body, speech and mind:
I now acknowledge it all, and repent.

我昔所造諸惡業，皆由無始貪恚癡，
從身語意之所生，一切我今皆懺悔。
