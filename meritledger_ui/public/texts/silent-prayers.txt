# Silent prayers

After chanting, in your own words and without hurry:

1. **Gratitude** for the Dharma, for the Lotus Sutra, and for those who carried it to you.
2. **Your own practice:** what you will change in yourself, starting today.
3. **Others:** the people in your life, by name; that they find their own way to awaken.
4. **Peace:** that the teaching spreads, and the world grows kinder because of it.
5. **Those who have died:** remember them, and carry their good forward.

Close with Nam-myoho-renge-kyo, three times.
