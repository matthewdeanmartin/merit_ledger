# The Three Refuges

I take refuge in the Buddha: the one who woke up, and showed that waking up is possible.

I take refuge in the Dharma: the way things are, and the teaching that shows it.

I take refuge in the Sangha: everyone who practises it, here and everywhere.

*Three times.*
